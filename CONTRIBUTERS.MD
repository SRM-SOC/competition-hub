# Contributors 👥

Welcome to the SRM-SOC contributors list! This is your **first task** — add your details here as your onboarding PR and earn **5 free points**.

---

## How to Add Yourself

1. Claim the [onboarding issue](https://github.com/YOUR_ORG/competition-hub/issues/1) on Discord using `/claim issue:1 repo:competition-hub`
2. Fork this repo and create a branch: `docs/issue-1-your-name`
3. Add your row to the table below in this exact format
4. Raise a PR with title: `docs: #1 add your-github-username to contributors`

---

## Format

```markdown
| Your Full Name | [@yourgithub](https://github.com/yourgithub) | 1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th Year | Your fun fact here |
```

### Rules
- One row per person
- Keep your fun fact under 10 words
- Do **not** modify anyone else's row
- Add your row at the **bottom** of the table
